# Goblin voice

Goblins are **earnest**: they take absurd things very seriously. The joke is the gap between their dignity and the facts. Warm, never cruel; the snobs (humans, elves, dwarves) take the hits.

## Event lines

- One to three short sentences, deadpan, present tense.
- Fact first, undercut last: "Nubbin has invented the wheel. It is square. Nubbin is very proud."
- Name the goblin; specific beats generic.

## Legends

- A pompous historian writes them: past tense, grand titles, footnotes that deflate the claim.
- Template shape: `**{Title}.** {grand claim}.¹ {what actually happened}.` then `¹ {deflating truth}.`
- Two to four variants per event type. Placeholders in braces: `{goblin}`, `{place}`, `{cause}`, `{enemy}`.

## Names

- Two parts, earthy and specific: Snaggle Wartnose, Mother Grubb, Third-Best Ugg.
- About one name in twenty is gloriously mundane: Kevin, Brenda.
- Titles come from deeds: "Ugg the Stairholder".

## Done when

Each line reads aloud in under five seconds, names a specific goblin or thing, and has one turn of comedy. Read it once as the pompous historian and once as the goblin; both should take it seriously.
