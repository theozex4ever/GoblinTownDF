# Glossary

One meaning per term. Use these words in code, tests, tickets, and commits. Add a term here before using a new one.

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Warren | The player's whole colony: tunnels, rooms, goblins. |
| Goblin | One simulated colonist, identified by its index in the goblin arrays. |
| Role | A goblin's job class: Grunt, Brute, Sneak, Tinkerer, Shaman, Boss. |
| Need | A 0–100 meter that drains over time. Prototype needs: Grub, Grog, Sleep. |
| Craving | The state of a goblin whose Need has fallen below the threshold. A Craving it can satisfy pulls it off its Job at once. |
| Starving | A goblin whose Grub Need is at 0. It walks and works at half speed; eating and drinking are not slowed, so a Starving goblin recovers as fast as any other. Grog at 0 has no such effect, though from P4 an unmet Need raises Chaos. |
| Collapse | What a goblin does when its Sleep Need reaches 0: it drops what it carries and sleeps on the spot until full, whatever else it was doing or craves, and nothing wakes it. A goblin with nowhere legal to put down its unit (every tile it can reach is a Pile full of that material) keeps it and collapses on the first tick a place opens. |
| Chaos | A goblin's accumulated frustration; high Chaos triggers acting out. In P4 it comes from unmet Needs, waiting for storage and having a claimed Haul cancelled by another goblin consuming supplies. It is held within 0 to 100, starts at 0, and fades slowly while no Need is below 30 and the goblin is not a waiting carrier. |
| Acting out | A chaos behaviour: shirk, fight, steal, set fire. A goblin may begin at 70 Chaos once it has no cooldown and is not meeting a Need. Finishing an act removes 40 Chaos and starts a cooldown of 300 ticks; an interrupted act gives no relief and starts the same cooldown. |
| Shirk | Act out by refusing available work and loafing on the spot. In P4 it lasts 100 ticks, abandons the Job (a dig keeps its progress, a Tend or Brew loses it) and puts down what the goblin carries by the same rules as a Craving. A satisfiable Craving or Collapse ends it early. |
| Fight | A nonlethal scuffle between two goblins that temporarily takes both away from work in P4. It runs in stages: the **instigator** (a goblin at 70 Chaos that chose to fight) is Pursuing its target for up to 100 ticks; on contact (the same tile or an orthogonal neighbour) the instigator is Scuffling and the **victim** is Scuffled for 30 ticks; a full scuffle leaves both Recovering for 100 ticks. The nearest reachable goblin on the instigator's level is the target, ties by goblin index; a goblin that is sleeping, seeking or satisfying a Need, acting out, in its cooldown or unable to legally put down its carried unit is not eligible, at choice or at contact. The victim gains 15 Chaos once, when the scuffle starts, and the instigator alone gets the act's relief when it ends. Both receive the 300-tick cooldown together when the scuffle ends, however it ends; a failed or interrupted pursuit gives it to the instigator alone. A satisfiable Craving or Collapse ends a scuffle for both with no relief and no recovery; one ends only that goblin's recovery. There is no health, injury or death. |
| Steal | Act out by taking one communal Grub or Grog from a Pile and consuming it privately a few steps away, even with no Craving. The thief is Sneaking to the nearest reachable Pile holding either (ties by tile index, Grog before Grub), Pilfering the unit for 5 ticks (the only moment stock leaves the Pile), MakingOff with it in hand to a tile that is no Pile on the source's level, up to four walking steps from it (the farthest, ties by tile index), and Scoffing it for 10 ticks. Finishing restores 50 to that Need, removes 40 Chaos and starts the 300-tick cooldown. Nothing is reserved, so the attempt fails if the unit is gone, the source stops being a Pile before the pickup, or the destination becomes a Pile or unreachable; no other destination is tried. A failure or a Need after the pickup puts the unit down by the Craving drop rule, and a thief with no legal place to put it is Stalled, holding the unit and waiting. There is no private stash and no Shinies. |
| Lost Haul | A Haul a goblin had claimed that another goblin cancelled by consuming supplies on the same tile. The displaced hauler gains Chaos once. Claims are quantities on a tile, not particular units, and a Pile painted over the tile or another hauler collecting a unit cancels a claim without annoying anyone. |
| Authority | The Boss's hold on the warren; low authority triggers a challenge. |
| Grudge | A goblin's remembered grievance against another goblin or faction. |
| Quirk | A fixed personality trait that bends a goblin's choices. |
| Band | A depth range of z-levels with shared materials and hazards: Surface, Dirt, Stone, Ore, The Deep. |
| Z-level | One horizontal layer of the map. 0 is the surface; higher numbers are deeper. |
| Tile | One cell of the map at an (x, y, z-level) position. Its kind is Solid, Floor, Open air, or Stairs. |
| Solid | An undug tile made of a material; nothing walks through it. Avoid: wall (kept for walls goblins build). |
| Floor | A tile goblins can walk on. |
| Open air | An empty tile with nothing to stand on. |
| Stairs | A walkable tile that links to a Stairs tile directly above or below it. |
| Material | A kind of unit that can be carried and stored: dirt, stone, Grub, Fungus, Grog. Solid tiles are made of dirt or stone, and digging one yields that material. |
| Grub | The food Material, and the Need it feeds. Comes from a Grub Pit. |
| Fungus | The Material brewed into Grog. Comes from a Fungus Farm. Not eaten. |
| Grog | The drink Material, and the Need it feeds. Brewed from Fungus in a Brewery. |
| Designation | A player order on tiles: Dig or Dig stairs. |
| Dig | A designation that turns a Solid tile into Floor. |
| Dig stairs | A designation that turns a Solid or Floor tile into Stairs. |
| Reachable | A Designation is reachable when some goblin can path to a tile it can dig from. An unreachable Designation waits: it stays designated, is skipped when goblins claim a Job, and shows greyed out until a dug tile opens a path. |
| Selection | A click-drag on the viewed level that picks a rectangle of tiles; a single click is a 1×1 rectangle. It does not know what the active tool will do with it. |
| Tool | The action a selection applies, picked by its key or its button in the Tool palette: Dig, Dig stairs, Pile, Grub Pit, Fungus Farm, Brewery, or Sleeping Heap. Right-drag removes what the active Tool makes. |
| Inspector | The HUD panel that shows what is under the cursor: every goblin on the tile, in index order, each with its name and Role, Task in plain words, Need meters with the Craving threshold marked, and Chaos with the acting-out threshold marked (the list scrolls inside the panel when the tile is crowded); every count on a Pile tile; or, for any other tile, what it is, what is marked on it and what lies loose there. It reads sim state and never writes it. |
| Warren summary | The HUD panel that totals the warren. Per material: units stored on Piles, and units loose, on the floor or in a goblin's hands. Goblins by state, each counted once, first match wins: asleep (Sleeping), craving (any Craving), idle (Idle, Wandering, Shirking or in any stage of a fight or a theft), or working. The Starving are also counted, whatever their state. It reads sim state and never writes it. |
| Cancel designation | The `CancelDesignation` Command: removes Designations from a rectangle of tiles. A tile mid-dig loses its progress and stays as it was. |
| Job | A unit of work in the global job list (dig, haul, tend, brew, build) that an idle goblin claims. |
| Tend | The Job of working a Grub Pit or Fungus Farm tile so that it produces. A Grub Pit tile yields several units per Tend, a Fungus Farm tile one. A Tend is offered on a tile that is not still spawning or growing and holds no loose unit of its crop, and one goblin works a tile at a time. Nearest first with digs and Hauls; there are no priorities. |
| Brew | The Job of taking one Fungus to a free Brewery tile and working it into one Grog. It sits at the Fungus, a Pile tile or a loose unit, on the one job list, nearest first by path with no priorities, and is offered only while a free Brewery tile and a Pile with room for the Grog are in reach of that Fungus. A Fungus unit has one claimant, a brewer or a hauler, and a Brewery tile one brewer; the Grog drops loose on the Brewery tile and is hauled to the nearest Pile like any other loose unit. A Brew waits while no Pile in reach has room for Grog, and its Brewery tile shows greyed out: an offered Brew is not started, and a brewer at work stops where it is, holding its Fungus and its progress, and carries on when room appears. A brewer's claim on a Fungus unit costs nothing to a hauler on any other unit of the tile. |
| Task | What one goblin is doing right now. A dig Job is worked as Walking to the claimed tile, then Digging it. A Haul is worked as Fetching (walking to the loose unit), PickingUp (5 ticks), Carrying (walking to the nearest Pile tile with room for its material, or waiting while none is available), and Dropping (5 ticks, which stores the unit). A Tend is worked as Approaching the claimed Grub Pit or Fungus Farm tile, then Tending it (20 ticks), after which the tile spawns its crop and the goblin is Idle. A Brew is worked as Gathering (walking to the claimed Fungus), Taking (5 ticks, which takes the unit off its tile or Pile), Bringing (carrying it to the nearest free Brewery tile, which it claims) and Brewing (20 working ticks, counted only while a Pile has room for the Grog, after which the Fungus is one Grog, loose on the tile, and the goblin is Idle). A Brewery cleared under a Bringing or Brewing goblin sends it Carrying the Fungus to a Pile, the brew's progress lost. A goblin with no Job is Idle, or Wandering. The Job is the work itself, and stays on the job list until it is done or cancelled; a carrying goblin is never given another Job until it has dropped its unit. A goblin with 70 Chaos and no cooldown may leave any of these for Shirking, loafing on the spot for 100 ticks and taking no Job, for a theft (Sneaking, Pilfering, MakingOff, Scoffing, or Stalled while it cannot put its stolen unit down; see Steal), or for a fight: Pursuing another goblin, then Scuffling (the instigator) or Scuffled (the victim, which keeps working until contact) for 30 ticks, then Recovering for 100 ticks, taking no Job in any of them. A Craving can end any of these Tasks at once, a Tend or a Brew losing its progress: the goblin puts down whatever it carries, goes Seeking the nearest reachable unit of Grub, then Eating it where it lies (10 ticks, no carrying), or SeekingGrog and Drinking for a Craving for Grog, or SeekingHeap (walking to the nearest reachable Sleeping Heap tile) and Sleeping there for a Craving for Sleep, which has no supply and is always met: with no Heap in reach the goblin is Sleeping where it stands. Sleeping lasts until the Sleep meter is full, however high it climbs past the Craving, and no other Need wakes it. With several Cravings the lowest meter wins, then Grub, Grog, Sleep in that order on a tie, and a Craving with nothing reachable to satisfy it is passed over; a goblin finishes the Need it has begun before it considers another, except that Collapse takes it off any of them. The Job it left stays on the list. |
| Wander | What an idle goblin does when it has no reachable Job: after a 3–6 second pause it walks to a random walkable tile within 4 tiles on its own z-level, then pauses again. A goblin mid-walk is Wandering. A reachable Designation cuts it short. |
| Pile | A storage area painted on Floor tiles with the Pile Tool. Each Pile tile holds up to 30 units of each material; different materials share a tile without a combined limit. |
| Loose item | A unit of material on a tile that is not a Pile, such as the one a dug tile drops. Each loose unit is a Haul Job, so a tile holding several (a removed Pile's stock, or dirt and stone together) is hauled one unit per trip and claimed by at most as many goblins as it has units. |
| Waiting carrier | A goblin holding a unit that no reachable Pile has room for. It stays put, still Carrying. Waiting raises its Chaos in P4, replacing P3's doubled Grog drain, until a Pile has room or it gives the unit up for a drink or a shirk. |
| Haul | The Job of carrying one loose unit to the nearest reachable Pile tile with room for its material. Without suitable storage, the unit waits on the ground or stays with its carrier until delivery is possible or a Craving makes the carrier drop it. |
| Room | A painted zone that works once it holds its required item: Sleeping Heap, Grub Pit, Fungus Farm, Brewery, Pile, Shrine, Boss Throne. The Pile needs no item, and no Room does until crafting and building exist. A tile holds one Room, so painting over a different Room skips that tile. |
| Sleeping Heap | The Room where goblins sleep. Any number can share a tile. A sleeper gains 1 point of Sleep per 5 ticks on a Heap and 1 per 10 anywhere else, following the tile under it now, and its Sleep does not drain while it sleeps. Painted on Floor like the Pile; shown as "Heap" on the Tool and in the key hints. |
| Grub Pit | The Room that produces Grub when Tended. One Tend of a tile takes 20 ticks and yields 3 Grub, the first 200 ticks after the Tend and one more every 200 ticks, each lying loose on the tile to be hauled. Removing the tile, or digging stairs through it, discards the Grub not yet spawned. |
| Fungus Farm | The Room that produces Fungus when Tended. Works only below the Surface: painting skips z0 tiles. One Tend of a tile takes 20 ticks and yields 1 Fungus, lying loose on the tile 300 ticks after the Tend. Removing the tile, or digging stairs through it, discards a Fungus still growing. |
| Brewery | The Room where a Brew Job turns Fungus into Grog. One brewer per tile. Painted on Floor at any depth like the Pile; shown as "Brewery" on the Tool and in the key hints. A Brewery tile is greyed out while no Pile in reach has room for Grog. |
| Raid | An abstracted off-map trip that returns loot and a story after a timer. |
| Anger | Surface factions' hostility; raised by raids, drives invasion size. |
| Invasion | Enemies entering the warren from the map edge or surface. |
| Legend | A chronicle entry written from a notable event. |
| Tick | One fixed sim step; 10 per second at normal speed. |
| Speed | How many ticks run per second of real time: 1×, 2× or 4×. Never changes the tick length. |
| Pause | A flag, separate from Speed, that stops ticks. Commands still apply, and Speed is kept for unpausing. |
| Command | An input from Godot to the sim. |
| Event | An output from the sim to Godot. |
| Tracer bullet | A thin, end-to-end, playable slice of a milestone. |
| Fun check | The five-question playtest verdict, logged in `docs/playtests/`. |
